Hold your hand palm-down

Sit 50–80 cm from the camera in good light, with your hand in frame.

Tap down, then lift

Each downward tap is one step. Lift your hand to re-arm. A faster tap gives a louder, brighter step.

Alternate hands

Your left hand is the left foot and your right hand is the right foot. Keys 1–4 change surface.

Hand tracking by MediaPipe. Footsteps are synthesized live with the Web Audio API. The Python version also sends MIDI to your DAW.
